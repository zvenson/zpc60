Make a beat

  1. REC (ARMED), PLAY: COUNT 4 3 2 1, then it records.
  2. Kick (white 1) and snare (white 2). AUTO CORRECT puts them on the grid.
  3. REC again at the end: an empty loop (BARS AUTO) is now as long as your take.
  4. Hats with NOTE REPEAT: REC, hold ENV and white 3: 1/16 hats while you hold. QUANT 1/8 or 1/32 for other rolls, SWING for the feel.
  5. Or step by step: hit the hat, hold SEQ: the white keys are its 16 steps; OCT+ / OCT− the next / last bar.
  6. Ghost notes: ARP twice (16 LEVELS), white 1–16 play the snare soft to loud.
  7. Another loop: a black key, record into it. Songs: SEQ → SONG.

Channels

8 channels, one sound each: a pad plays on its place in the row pair: pads 01 and 09 on channel 1, 02 and 10 on 2 … 08 and 16 on 8 (in both banks). A new hit on a channel cuts the last: HAT (03) and OHAT (11) choke. Channels 1–2 (pads 01 02 09 10) have the resonant filter, 3–6 a fixed low-pass, 7–8 none.

Saving and safety

Savedby itself when stopped and 3 s after a change; SAVE now
Its ownzpc60 keeps its own save; it never reads zp12's (they share the flash: switching back and forth, the last one saving wins)
Not savedTRACK MUTE, mute / solo and the DJ filter
UndoEDIT held + OCT−: the last clear, copy or stopped erase
RESETGLO → SETUP: everything back to the factory, all loops gone
Rescueno answer? Hold OCT− while switching on: the USB rescue, then install again
